Frequently asked
How do I get verified (KYC)?

Open Account → Identity verification and upload a valid ID. Our team reviews it, usually within 24 hours.

How does payment & escrow work?

When you book, your money is held safely in escrow and only released to the provider once you confirm the job is done.

How do I start selling or offering a service?

Open Account → Sell or offer a service, pick what you do, set up your shop or profile, and you go live on the marketplace.
